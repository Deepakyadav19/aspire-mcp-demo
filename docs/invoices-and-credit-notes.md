# Invoices and credit notes

## Issuing an invoice
A draft invoice can be edited freely. When it is issued it receives the next invoice number and can no longer be edited. Issuing posts the receivable and the revenue to the ledger.

## Correcting an issued invoice
An issued invoice is never edited. To reduce the amount, issue a credit note that references the original invoice. To cancel it completely, void the invoice, which posts a reversal entry.

## Partial payments
When a customer pays only part of an invoice, the invoice status becomes partially paid and the open amount is reduced. The remaining amount stays in accounts receivable until it is paid or written off.
