# Month-end close checklist

## Reconcile bank accounts
Match every bank statement line to a ledger entry. Investigate unmatched items, such as missing bills, duplicate entries or bank fees that were never recorded.

## Review uncategorised transactions
Clear the uncategorised or suspense account by assigning each transaction to the correct expense or income account and tax code.

## Revalue foreign currency balances
Revalue open foreign currency invoices, bills and bank balances at the month-end exchange rate. The difference is recorded as an unrealised foreign exchange gain or loss.

## Lock the period
After the review, lock the period so that no entries can be posted to it. Corrections after locking are made in the next open period with a clear description.
