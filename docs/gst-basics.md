# GST basics for Singapore businesses

## What GST is
Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a tax on the supply of goods and services in Singapore and on imports. The standard rate is 9% from 1 January 2024. A GST-registered business charges GST on its sales (output tax) and can usually claim back the GST it pays on business purchases (input tax).

## When a business must register
Registration is compulsory when taxable turnover is more than S$1 million in the past 12 months, or is expected to be more than S$1 million in the next 12 months. Businesses below the threshold can choose to register voluntarily.

## Claiming input tax
Input tax can be claimed when the business is GST-registered, the purchase is for business purposes, and the business holds a valid tax invoice or receipt. Some expenses are disallowed even when they are business related, for example club subscription fees and expenses for private cars.

## Filing GST returns
GST-registered businesses file a GST return, called GST F5, usually every quarter. The return reports output tax, input tax and the net amount payable to or refundable from IRAS.
