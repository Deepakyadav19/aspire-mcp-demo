# Expense categories in the chart of accounts

## Software and subscriptions
Recurring software tools such as cloud hosting, design tools and productivity suites go to Software and Subscriptions. Annual plans paid upfront can be recorded as a prepaid expense and recognised monthly.

## Travel and meals
Flights, hotels and ground transport for business trips go to Travel. Meals with clients go to Entertainment, while staff meals during overtime may go to Staff Welfare. Keep the receipt and note the business purpose.

## Office and equipment
Small items like stationery go to Office Supplies. Equipment above the company's capitalisation threshold, for example laptops, is recorded as a fixed asset and depreciated instead of being expensed at once.

## Bank charges
Bank fees, card processing fees and foreign transaction fees go to Bank Charges. They are usually not subject to GST.
