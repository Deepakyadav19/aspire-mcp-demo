# Foreign exchange gains and losses

## Realised gains and losses
When an invoice in a foreign currency is paid at a different exchange rate from the rate on the invoice date, the difference is a realised foreign exchange gain or loss. For example, a USD invoice booked at 1.35 and paid at 1.33 produces a loss in SGD.

## Unrealised gains and losses
At month end, open foreign currency balances are revalued at the closing rate. The difference is an unrealised gain or loss, which can reverse in later months.

## Which rate to use
Use a consistent and documented source of exchange rates, record the rate used on each transaction, and keep the original currency amount alongside the converted amount.
